# Featured Projects

## 🏥 MeduzaAI

AI-powered Patient Queue & Clinic CRM

MeduzaAI helps hospitals reduce waiting time by collecting patient information before the consultation begins.

Patients scan a QR code, complete an AI-assisted interview, and doctors receive structured medical information before seeing the patient.

The platform also includes Telegram notifications, doctor dashboards, and real-time queue management.

**Tech**

`NestJS`
`PostgreSQL`
`Prisma`
`Socket.IO`
`Cloudinary`
`Telegram Bot`
`LLM Integration`

## 💼 Job Board

A modern two-sided employment platform.

Unlike traditional job boards where only companies publish vacancies, Job Board allows both employers and job seekers to create listings.

Companies can publish jobs, while individuals can advertise their own skills and availability.
